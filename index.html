01 About Waterly

Waterly is designed for personal fluid intake tracking, daily goal management, reminders, and intake history.

Waterly is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any disease or medical condition.

02 Information processed by Waterly

Waterly processes information that you choose to enter or configure in the app, including:

  • drink entries and their volumes;
  • quick drink presets and their volumes;
  • daily fluid intake goal;
  • reminder settings;
  • theme and language preferences;
  • onboarding state and other app settings.

03 Local storage

In Waterly 1.0, the information listed above is stored locally on your device.

Waterly does not require you to create an account.

Waterly does not use its own cloud service to synchronize your drink records or app settings.

04 Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Waterly 1.0 does not contain advertising SDKs.

Waterly 1.0 does not use analytics, telemetry, attribution, crash-reporting, advertising, or behavioral tracking SDKs.

Waterly does not sell user data to advertisers.

05 Network access and data transmission

Waterly 1.0 does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not contain an embedded network client for transmitting drink records or app settings.

Waterly itself does not upload your drink records or app settings to any server.

However, some actions initiated by you may open or pass information to another application on your device. Those external applications operate under their own privacy policies.

06 Notifications and permissions

Waterly may request permission to send notifications so that it can provide local drink reminders.

Waterly also uses the Android permission required to restore scheduled local reminders after the device restarts.

In Waterly 1.0, the release build uses only:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show local reminders on supported Android versions;
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore local reminder scheduling after a device reboot.

Waterly 1.0 does not request permissions for location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone calls, SMS, shared storage, or advertising ID.

07 Data export and sharing

Waterly allows you to export data as a CSV file through the Android system share interface.

Export occurs only when you explicitly initiate it.

When you choose another application or service in the Android share interface, the exported data may leave your device and be processed by that selected application or service.

Revol Apps does not control how third-party applications selected by you process exported data. Please review the privacy policy of the application or service you choose.

08 External links and email

Waterly may open the Privacy Policy in your external web browser.

Waterly may also open your device's email application when you choose to contact the developer.

Your browser, email application, or other external applications may process information according to their own privacy policies.

09 Backups and device transfer

Waterly 1.0 disables Android application backup and excludes Waterly application storage from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer rules configured by the app.

This does not control backups created independently by the user or by tools outside Waterly's control.

10 Deleting your data

You can remove drink records using the functions provided by Waterly.

Because Waterly 1.0 stores its app data locally, uninstalling Waterly removes its local application data from the device, subject to Android system behavior and any copies that you previously exported or created outside the app.

11 Third parties

Waterly 1.0 does not contain third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, payments, cloud storage, or telemetry.

If you choose to open a browser, email application, or share exported data with another application, that third party may process information independently of Waterly.

12 Children's privacy

Waterly is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Waterly 1.0 does not require account registration and does not ask users to provide their name or email address as part of normal app use.

13 Security

Waterly is designed to minimize data processing and to keep app records locally on the user's device whenever possible.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and any applications or services to which you choose to export data.

14 Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Waterly's features, permissions, data handling, or third-party integrations change.

If advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, accounts, or other data-processing services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before or together with that release.
